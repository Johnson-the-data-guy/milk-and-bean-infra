Our story

A little counter with big mornings.

Milk & Bean opened as a two-table counter with one goal: pour coffee worth slowing down for. We roast in small batches, bake before sunrise, and know most of our regulars by their order.

No rush, no gimmicks — just good coffee, warm bread, and a seat by the window whenever you need one.
